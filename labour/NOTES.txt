Netherlands - labour authority: UWV (Uitvoeringsinstituut Werknemersverzekeringen)
Collected 14-15 November 2026. Script: download_nl_labour.py (python3 download_nl_labour.py [ruwav2022 ruwav2014 delegatie uwv2018 uwv2009 uwvwoo]).

Role
UWV decides applications for a work permit (tewerkstellingsvergunning, TWV) under the Foreign Nationals
Employment Act (Wet arbeid vreemdelingen, Wav) and advises the IND on the labour-market part of combined
permits (GVVA) and, on request, on whether the salary of a knowledge migrant or EU Blue Card applicant is
at market rate. The IND decides the EU Blue Card; no TWV is needed for it (art. 2.2 BuWav 2022).

Temporary-work agencies and the EU Blue Card in these texts
- Regeling uitvoering Wav 2022, annex I, para. 2.1 (on art. 2 Wav, "Tewerkstellingsvergunning of gecombineerde
  vergunning"): "Het ter beschikking stellen van arbeidskrachten blijft onder de tewerkstellingsvergunning vallen
  (met uitzondering van de gevallen waarop de artikelen 56 en 57 VwEU van toepassing zijn ...). De uitzendende of
  inlenende werkgever dient een tewerkstellingsvergunning aan te vragen." Intra-group secondment, collegial lending
  without profit and contracting for work (aanneming van werk) are not hiring-out. The same text is in the 2014
  regulation.
- Regeling uitvoering Wav 2022, annex I, para. 8.3.b.17: TWV for a long-term resident who formerly held an EU Blue
  Card.
- UWV Werkinstructie adviesverzoeken marktconform loon (kennismigranten/Europese blauwe kaart), section 3,
  "Aandacht: beoordeling bij feitelijke werkgever": where the knowledge migrant is employed by one employer
  (formele werkgever / uitlener) and hired out to a third party (feitelijk werkgever / inlener), "De beoordeling
  van de marktconformiteit vindt plaats bij de feitelijk werkgever. Deze situatie neem je expliciet op in het
  advies." The same instruction covers the EU Blue Card salary criterion (art. 2.2(1)(b) BuWav). The handbook page
  (annex 2) repeats it.
- No text names payroll companies or employers of record as such.

How much real guidance
UWV's working rules are mostly published as law: the ministerial implementing regulation, whose annex is a
detailed decision rulebook, and UWV's own policy rules, all in every historical version. UWV's internal
handbook ("Wetsuitleg Werkvergunningen") and work instructions are not published; the only parts public are the
pages released under the Woo in 2025 on market-rate salary for knowledge migrants and Blue Card holders.
