# Immigration guidance: the Netherlands

Published guidance that the authorities of the Netherlands issue to the officials who decide immigration cases, collected in November 2026.

- **Guidance:** 294 files in this folder. `index.tsv` lists each file with its title, date and source address.
- **Country-of-origin reports:** 68 files in `coi/`, with their own `coi/index.tsv`.
- The files are unmodified copies of what the source published. Where a source refused automated access, copies come from a public web archive; the index says which.
- What is missing, and how to obtain it, is described in [MISSING.md](https://github.com/immigration-guidelines/.github/blob/main/MISSING.md), in the repository that also holds the download scripts. Other jurisdictions are listed on the [organisation page](https://github.com/immigration-guidelines).

**Labour authority.** `labour/` holds the texts UWV applies in deciding work permits under the Foreign Nationals Employment Act (Wav): every version of the implementing regulation and its predecessor and of the delegation decision (procedural acts), UWV's policy rules of 2009 and 2018, and ten documents released by UWV under the Open Government Act in 2025, among them its work instruction on the market-rate salary test for highly skilled migrants and EU Blue Card holders. Personal data in the released files was blacked out by UWV.

The documents remain the work of the authorities that published them.
